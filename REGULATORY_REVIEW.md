# Regulatory and methodology review contract

This contract governs use of hard-coded dates, thresholds and legal/methodology
claims in the historical source catalogue. Those claims are not certified by a
prompt, XML wrapper, source locator or successful software test.

For every material instrument, record the jurisdiction, affected entity/product,
exact instrument/version and provision, authoritative source URL, status, adoption/
entry-into-force/application timing where relevant, analysis cutoff, reviewer and
last-review date. The starter register has only an `effective_date` field for the
operative applicability check; maintain the distinct other dates in the supporting
evidence record rather than conflating them. Expand the inventory for the task.

Statuses are unverified, proposal, adopted_not_applicable, applicable or superseded.
A proposal may be a scenario, not an operative obligation. An adopted measure may
have future or phased application. Do not assume a source-task statement is current
merely because it contains a recent year. Source and interpret the relevant scope.

Require human review of firm-specific binding elements, legal applicability,
threshold selection, source conflicts and marketing-facing claims. Separate
regulatory requirements, voluntary methodologies, vendor definitions and fund
policy. Missing data and unclear scope must remain visible.

The default 90-day review interval is a maintenance-policy choice, not a legal
safe harbor. Material developments warrant earlier review. The register checker
validates recorded structure and dates only; it never authenticates source text
or certifies compliance. All initial entries are intentionally unverified.

Every output should include source IDs, numeric units, data vintage, jurisdiction,
methodology version, unresolved discrepancies, and whether qualified review is
complete. Do not issue a final law-dependent compliance conclusion while required
records remain unverified. Do not invent affiliations, source access or evidence.
